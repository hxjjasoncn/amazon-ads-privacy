Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Amazon Ads Internal Tool handles information obtained through the Amazon Ads API.

1. Purpose

This application is an internal business tool used to access, analyze, and optimize Amazon advertising accounts owned or operated by our company.

2. Information We Access

The application may access advertising account information, campaigns, ad groups, keywords, targeting data, search terms, and advertising performance metrics.

3. How We Use Information

The information is used only for internal advertising analysis, reporting, campaign optimization, bid and budget analysis, and keyword and targeting optimization.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell Amazon advertising data and do not share it with third parties except where required to operate the application securely or by law.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect advertising data and authentication credentials. OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens are stored securely and are not intentionally exposed publicly.

6. Data Retention

Advertising data is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for internal analysis, reporting, historical comparison, and optimization.

7. Third-Party Services

This application uses the Amazon Ads API and Login with Amazon services and is subject to Amazon's applicable terms and policies.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be published on this page.
